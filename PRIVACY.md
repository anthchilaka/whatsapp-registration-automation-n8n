# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 5, 2026

This policy covers the WhatsApp member-registration assistant built for Legion of Mary's CIC Comitium ("the bot," "we," "us"), operated by **Anthony Chilaka (anthonychilaka.com)**, available on WhatsApp.

**What the bot collects**

When you register with the bot, we collect:

- Your title, first name, last name, and the office you hold in the Comitium.
- Your month and day of birth.
- Your phone number, from your WhatsApp account.
- Your preferred language, detected from how you write to us.
- A photo you send us directly in the chat.

We don't ask for or collect anything beyond what's needed to complete your registration. We don't request payment details, passwords, or identity documents through the bot.

**How we use it**

We use this to register you as a member: to confirm your details back to you, to save your record, and — going forward — to recognize your birthday and keep in touch about Comitium activities. Nothing you tell the bot is used for anything beyond your registration and the Comitium's own membership records.

**How long we keep it**

Technical logs (records of each automated run) are deleted automatically after **14 days**. Your registration record, including your photo, is kept as part of the Comitium's membership records for as long as you remain a member, unless you ask us to correct or delete it sooner.

**Who has access**

The bot's core hosting (the server and database that run the conversation) is infrastructure we operate directly, not a third-party platform. A small number of specific services process your data as part of registering you: OpenAI checks an answer only when it doesn't clearly match an expected format, and Airtable stores your finished registration record. Each only processes what's needed to do that one job; none of them are data brokers or advertisers, and we don't sell your data to anyone. WhatsApp, as the messaging platform the bot runs on, handles message delivery under its own privacy policy, which applies alongside this one.

**Your rights**

If you're in Nigeria, this processing falls under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting **services@anthonychilaka.com**.

**Changes to this policy**

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the effective date above and post the new version at this same link.
